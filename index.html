⏳ From Rosario to Immortality (1987 – 2026)

2000 – 2005 The Napkin Contract & First Senior Goal
On December 14, 2000, Carles Rexach signed a commitment on a paper napkin in Barcelona to recruit 13-year-old Messi. He made his official La Liga debut on October 16, 2004 against Espanyol and scored his first senior goal on May 1, 2005 against Albacete off a Ronaldinho lobbed assist, before winning the 2005 FIFA U-20 World Cup Golden Ball and Golden Shoe.
2008 – 2009 The Number 10 Shirt, The False 9 & The Sextuple
Inheriting the iconic #10 shirt under Pep Guardiola, Messi debuted the "False 9" role in the historic 6–2 victory at the Santiago Bernabéu, scored the towering header against Manchester United in the 2009 Rome UCL Final, and led Barcelona to an unprecedented six-trophy calendar year alongside his first Ballon d'Or.
2011 – 2012 Wembley Masterclass & 91 Goals in a Calendar Year
Following a dominant 2011 Champions League final display at Wembley against Manchester United, Messi recorded 50 La Liga goals in the 2011–12 season and 91 goals across all competitions in 2012—earning his fourth consecutive Ballon d'Or at just 25 years old.
2014 – 2015 The MSN Treble & Second Champions League Crown as Talisman
Forming the legendary "MSN" trident alongside Luis Suárez and Neymar (122 combined goals in 2014–15), Messi dismantled Bayern Munich in the UCL semifinals and delivered Barcelona's second European Treble in Berlin, claiming his 5th Ballon d'Or.
2021 – 2022 Maracanã Liberation & The Lusail Coronation
In July 2021, Messi ended Argentina's 28-year trophy drought by winning the Copa América at the Maracanã. After sweeping Italy 3–0 in the 2022 Finalissima at Wembley, he produced a tournament for the ages at Qatar 2022—scoring in every knockout round and netting twice in the final against France to lift the FIFA World Cup.
2023 – 2026 8th Ballon d'Or, Back-to-Back Copa América & Miami Dynasty
After capturing a record-extending 8th Ballon d'Or in October 2023 and defending Argentina's Copa América crown in 2024, Messi transformed Inter Miami CF into a powerhouse—capturing the Leagues Cup, the Supporters' Shield, the 2025 MLS Title, and the 2026 Campeones Cup while surpassing 100 goals for the club.
